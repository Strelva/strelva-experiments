# Buffalo action case

## Who this helps

Buffalo and Erie County residents, tenants, small property owners, and
community advocates who receive a notice, summons, hearing date, denial, or
other agency communication and need to organize the next factual step.

## Immediate-use workflow

Keep the entire notice and envelope or electronic header. Identify the issuing
agency, case or parcel number, issue date, service date, stated response date,
hearing date, payment or filing instructions, and the contact printed on the
notice. Use `SKILL.md` to build a fact timeline, verify the current agency
procedure, and make a dated contact log. Treat every deadline printed on the
notice as urgent and confirm it with the issuing office. If the notice is a
City administrative summons, start with the City's hearing information. If it
is a FOIL denial, use the relevant records officer and appeal guidance. For a
zoning decision, use the City's administrative-appeal form and instructions.

## Limits

This pack does not decide whether a notice is valid, calculate a person's legal
deadline, choose a defense, or act as a lawyer or representative. Agency pages
can change and a notice may control even when a general webpage differs. Never
miss a stated date while waiting for this pack. Contact the issuing agency and,
when the stakes are significant, a qualified lawyer or accredited advocate.

## Update method

Review all procedure and contact URLs quarterly and before using them. Test one
recent City hearing page, one FOIL appeal page, and one State or federal appeal
page. Update `sources.json`, `pack.json` `reviewedAt`, and this README whenever
an agency changes its form, contact, deadline language, or scope. Keep dated
copies of notices and old procedures in the case file.
