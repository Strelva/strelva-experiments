# Understand a Buffalo notice

## Safety rule

This is an organizing method, not legal advice. Do not wait for research before
responding to a date printed on a notice. Call the issuing agency using a number
on the notice or its current official page. Ask whether the date, hearing,
payment route, or appeal route has changed, and record the answer and staff
name if provided.

## Input

- The complete notice, summons, denial, order, or email, including envelope or
  electronic header when available.
- Any attachments, photographs, prior correspondence, and proof of delivery.
- A notes file and a calendar. The calendar is for reminders, not a legal
  calculation.

## Procedure

1. Make a read-only copy of the notice. Name it with the received date and do
   not edit the original. Note whether the copy is complete or missing pages.
2. Transcribe, without interpretation: issuing agency, recipient, property or
   case number, notice/order type, issue date, service or received date, stated
   response date, hearing date, amount if shown, and every contact or filing
   instruction.
3. Highlight four separate dates: **document date**, **service/received date**,
   **stated response date**, and **hearing date**. Never replace a missing date
   with an estimate.
4. Classify the route as one of: City administrative adjudication, City zoning,
   City/County FOIL, County Social Services fair hearing, assessment, housing,
   State agency, federal agency, or unknown. If unknown, call the issuer before
   choosing a route.
5. Open the matching official source in `sources.json`. Compare the current
   procedure, contact, form, and terminology with the notice. Record any
   mismatch as a question for the issuer, not as proof that the notice is wrong.
6. Contact the issuer using a verifiable channel. Ask only factual process
   questions: receipt, case status, where to file, acceptable format, hearing
   access, and whether the printed date remains in effect. Request written
   confirmation when possible.
7. Preserve evidence in a simple log. Keep sent messages, delivery receipts,
   call notes, screenshots, filenames, and official URLs. Do not publish private
   identifiers unless necessary and authorized.
8. Set calendar reminders for the notice's stated dates and for a follow-up
   contact. Label each reminder **date copied from notice** or **agency-confirmed**.
9. Escalate when needed. If the agency cannot explain the process, the issue
   affects housing, benefits, property, immigration, safety, or a large sum, or
   a hearing may be missed, contact a qualified lawyer, legal-aid organization,
   or accredited advocate promptly.

## Case worksheet

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Issuer and office | Exact agency and division |
| Notice type | Exact title or heading |
| Case/property ID | Exact number, SBL, or account |
| Document date | Date printed on notice |
| Received/service date | How and when it arrived |
| Stated response date | Copy exactly, with timezone or business-day wording |
| Hearing date/location | Copy exactly or write not stated |
| Filing/payment route | Mail, portal, email, in person, or not stated |
| Questions to issuer | Short factual questions only |
| Contact log | Date, channel, person/office, answer, confirmation |
| Evidence files | Filename and what it proves |
| Professional help | Who was contacted and when |

## Route notes

- **City administrative adjudication:** use the City's hearing-information
  page and the notice. The City page discusses hearing requests, defaults,
  payment, and appeals, but it cannot set a case-specific date.
- **City zoning:** use the current Administrative Appeal Form and ask the
  Zoning Administrator or Board of Appeals to confirm completeness, fee, and
  hearing status.
- **FOIL denial:** preserve the original request and written denial. Identify
  the records withheld and follow the responsible office's appeal instructions.
  Do not assume a general State page names the correct local officer.
- **County Social Services fair hearing:** follow the Notice of Intent exactly,
  preserve fax or postmark proof, and ask about aid-continuing instructions.
- **Assessment or housing issue:** use the State or federal source only to find
  the correct agency route. The notice and local agency determine the case.
- **Unknown:** do not guess. Search the issuer's official domain and call the
  office listed on the document.

## Output contract

Return:

- a one-page fact timeline;
- the exact dates and identifiers copied from the notice;
- a source-linked process check;
- a contact and evidence log;
- the next factual action and its confirmation status;
- an explicit warning that the method does not calculate deadlines or provide
  legal advice.

## Failure handling

- If the notice is incomplete or illegible, list the missing page or field and
  request a complete copy from the issuer.
- If the deadline is unclear, write **unclear** and ask the issuer or a qualified
  professional. Do not infer a date from a general webpage.
- If the agency portal rejects a submission, save the error, try the alternate
  official channel printed on the notice, and record both attempts.
- If a source is unavailable, use the agency's current landing page and direct
  contact. Do not rely on a search snippet or an unofficial summary.
- If the issue is urgent or safety-related, prioritize a human professional and
  emergency services where appropriate over further source research.
