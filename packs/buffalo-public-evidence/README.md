# Buffalo public evidence

## Who this helps

Residents, journalists, public-interest researchers, neighborhood groups, and
small organizations that need to follow a Buffalo or Erie County decision from
notice through vote, record, and follow-up.

## Immediate-use workflow

Start with one concrete claim or decision and a date range. Find the official
meeting or record page, capture the agenda item or record identifier, then
compare the agenda, minutes, recording, and any supporting public dataset. Keep
facts, attributed statements, and your own interpretation in separate columns.
If a needed record is not published, use the relevant FOIL path and log the
request rather than implying the record does not exist.

## Limits

Agendas, minutes, recordings, and open-data rows can be posted later, corrected,
or incomplete. Minutes are not a transcript. A meeting notice is not a vote.
An open-data row is not proof of motive, causation, or wrongdoing. Public
records may contain redactions, personal information, or errors. Do not publish
private personal data merely because it is accessible.

This pack does not authenticate a claim for court, determine a legal violation,
or provide legal, investigative, or journalistic advice.

## Update method

Check each landing page quarterly and before relying on a meeting date, FOIL
procedure, or dataset definition. Recheck one recent agenda and one older item
to catch archive changes. Update `sources.json` and `pack.json` `reviewedAt` when
publisher, URL, coverage, or limits change. Keep a dated copy or citation for
every published claim.
